HEICO Vs. TransDigm: Whose Aerospace Monopoly Is Better?
========================================================
URL:  https://www.theinvestorspodcast.com/episodes/heico-vs-transdigm-whose-aerospace-monopoly-is-better-tip/
Date: 03 October 2026

In today’s episode, Kyle Grieve and Shawn O’Malley discuss HEICO and TransDigm, two dominant players in the aerospace components industry known for their pricing power and resiliency. They’ll compare how each company grows through acquisitions and organic expansion, how they approach debt and capital allocation, and what gives each business its durable competitive advantages. The discussion also covers the risks facing both companies, including regulatory scrutiny and reliance on continued aerospace demand, as well as potential sources of future growth.

IN THIS EPISODE, YOU’LL LEARN:
Why the aerospace industry has such high barriers to entry
How TransDigm built its sole-source pricing power model
How HEICO competes by underpricing original equipment manufacturers
Why both companies rely heavily on acquisitions to grow
How each company’s approach to debt differs sharply
What gives HEICO and TransDigm such durable competitive advantages
Why regulators have started scrutinizing TransDigm’s acquisition strategy
How ownership and incentive structures shape each management team
What risks could disrupt either company’s long runway of growth
Where future growth could come from for both businesses
