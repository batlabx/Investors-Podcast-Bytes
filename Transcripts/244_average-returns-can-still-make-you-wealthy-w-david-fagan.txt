In this episode, Stig Brodersen speaks with David Fagan about Total Wealth Performance and why someone with average investment returns can still build a meaningful net worth. They discuss why saving is the first step in wealth creation, why financial independence is a matter of intention rather than luck, and how idle cash quietly becomes one of the most expensive decisions investors ever make.

SUBSCRIBE

IN THIS EPISODE, YOU’LL LEARN:
Why David has been a saver ever since he blew his entire net worth on a Hot Wheels racetrack at age eight
Why money decisions that look odd are often rational given someone’s lived experiences
Why financial independence is not luck but small habits that compound
What Total Wealth Performance is, and why the return on your broker statement is not your real return
The ABCs of front-end performance: availability, behavior, and configuration
Why idle cash is one of the most expensive decisions an investor can make
How Stig thinks about financial independence
Why net worth beats portfolio returns as a scorecard during the accumulation phase
How and why tax is a real expense 
And much, much more!
