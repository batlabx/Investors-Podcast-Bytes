Stock Picker: How to Live Off Your Portfolio w/ Ian Cassel
==========================================================

In this episode, Stig Brodersen welcomes back Ian Cassel, founder of MicroCapClub and CIO of Intelligent Fanatics Capital Management, to discuss his new book, Stock Picker. They dig into why most microcaps must be sold within 36 months, why Ian never holds a large cash position, and how he arrived at the $2 million that let him live off his portfolio.

SUBSCRIBE

IN THIS EPISODE, YOU’LL LEARN:
Why Ian’s first big win at 16  shaped his risk tolerance for life.
How Ian arrived at the $2 million he needed to become a full-time private investor, and why the number was about pain tolerance, not expected returns.
Why there is no such thing as saving when you live off your portfolio, and the safeguards Ian built to survive consistently inconsistent returns.
Why most microcaps you buy must be sold within 36 months, even the winners, and why the greats had their best returns in their highest-turnover years.
Why Ian never holds a large cash position, and how a 3 to 5% cash buffer forces him to sell his least convicted idea.
Whether Ian would take a guaranteed 20% annual return for the rest of his life.
And much, much more!
