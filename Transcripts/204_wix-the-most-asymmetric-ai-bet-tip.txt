WIX: The Most Asymmetric AI Bet?
========================================
URL:  https://www.theinvestorspodcast.com/episodes/wix-the-most-asymmetric-ai-bet-tip/
Date: 03 June 2026

Daniel Mahncke and Shawn O’Malley take a deep dive into Wix.com — the Israeli website-building platform whose investment case now turns on two of the most debated questions in the stock today: whether the generative-AI wave that lets anyone spin up a site from a text prompt is the end of Wix or whether Wix is too sticky, and whether the Base 44 acquisition — Wix’s bet on AI-powered app generation — is the next leg of the story or a distraction from the SMB infrastructure business the company already dominates.
Some investors believe Wix is set up to compound for the next decade, with a recurring subscription base that behaves more like embedded small-business infrastructure than a discretionary website tool, retention that holds because switching means rebuilding a company’s entire online presence, and an AI roadmap — from the Wix-built LLM powering Harmony to Base 44’s vibe-coding engine — that the market is pricing at roughly zero. Others see a more uncomfortable picture. The declining subscriber count looks less like deliberate, margin-accretive pruning of unprofitable customers and more like the leading edge of AI disruption, while the Base 44 push runs Wix straight into a crowded field of well-funded AI app builders with no obvious durable moat.
Join Daniel Mahncke and Shawn O’Malley as they work through whether Wix’s subscription flywheel is strengthening or being quietly eroded by AI-native entrants, examine what the company’s AI strategy actually looks like in 2026 versus the “website builder in terminal decline” narrative the market still anchors to, and assess whether Wix.com deserves a spot in The Intrinsic Value Portfolio.
