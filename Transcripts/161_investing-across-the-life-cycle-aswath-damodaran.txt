Investing Across the Life Cycle w/ Aswath Damodaran
===================================================

On today’s episode, Clay Finck is joined by Aswath Damodaran to discuss his new book, The Corporate Life Cycle.
Aswath Damodaran is a professor at NYU of corporate finance and valuation and has taught thousands of students how to value companies and pick stocks. He has written numerous books on valuation and has also made all of his university courses available online for free. Aswath is one of the clearest teachers of finance and investing in the industry.

IN THIS EPISODE, YOU’LL LEARN:
What the corporate life cycle is and why it’s important.
Why value investors should be open to investing at different points in the life cycle.
Why the job of management is to maximize shareholder value.
The difference in returns between value and growth investing.
How the story and narrative around Tesla has changed over the past decade.
How management’s skillset needs to change throughout the corporate life cycle.
How companies should handle the decline phase of the life cycle.
Why Aswath is against concentrating his portfolio.
And so much more!
