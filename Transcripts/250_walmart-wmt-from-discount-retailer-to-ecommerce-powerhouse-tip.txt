Walmart: From Discount Retailer to eCommerce Powerhouse
=======================================================
URL:  https://www.theinvestorspodcast.com/episodes/walmart-wmt-from-discount-retailer-to-ecommerce-powerhouse-tip/
Date: 30 September 2026

In today’s episode, Kyle Grieve and Shawn O’Malley discuss Walmart and how it grew from Sam Walton’s small-town five-and-dime into a retail giant. Today, it also makes money from e-commerce, a marketplace, advertising and memberships. They cover the scale advantages behind Walmart’s everyday low prices, how its suppliers end up financing its inventory, and why its power over those suppliers continues to draw antitrust scrutiny. They also look at Walmart’s capital allocation, management, growth levers and the biggest risks facing the business.
