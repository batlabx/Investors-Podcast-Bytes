Why Hari is bullish on Meta (Ticker: META), highlighting its advertising dominance, network effects, and long-term monetization potential.
The bear case for Meta, including massive AI infrastructure spending, uncertain returns on capital, and execution risk around AI monetization
Why Tobias is bullish on Booking Holdings (Ticker: BKNG), emphasizing its capital-light business model and robust travel ecosystem.

Why Stig is bullish on Adobe, focusing on its switching costs and subscription-based revenue model (Ticker: NASDAQ: ADBE)
The bear case for Adobe, including AI-generated content and the increasing competition from tools like Canva and LLMs.
