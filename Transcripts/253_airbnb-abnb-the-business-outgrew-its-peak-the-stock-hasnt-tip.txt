In today’s episode, Kyle Grieve and Shawn O’Malley discuss whether Airbnb’s recent slowdown was a warning sign or just building the groundwork for its next growth phase. They explore how a company that owns no homes built one of travel’s strongest brands, and why AI agents could threaten or strengthen it. You’ll also learn how Brian Chesky’s founder-led approach and new growth levers are shaping the business’s future.
