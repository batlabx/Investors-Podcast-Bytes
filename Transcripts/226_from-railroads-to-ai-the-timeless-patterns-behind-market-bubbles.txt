The Timeless Patterns Behind Market Bubbles
===========================================
URL:  https://www.theinvestorspodcast.com/episodes/from-railroads-to-ai-the-timeless-patterns-behind-market-bubbles/
Date: 13 June 2026

On today’s episode, Kyle Grieve discusses what bubbles are, why they form, and why they always feel different in real time. He’ll examine historical patterns through frameworks from Insana, Kindleberger, and Howard Marks, and explain how investors can protect themselves by focusing on intrinsic value over narratives rather than speculation.

SUBSCRIBE

IN THIS EPISODE, YOU’LL LEARN:
Why understanding bubbles is critical for long-term investor survival
How “this time is different” fuels every historical bubble
Why smart money, incentives, and career risk inflate bubbles
How investors rationalize bubbles using new, useless KPIs
The predictable emotional arc: skepticism, euphoria, panic, collapse
Kindleberger’s five stages: displacement, boom, revulsion, discredit
Why price detaches from intrinsic value during bubbles
Lessons from tiny bubbles like plank roads and Beanie Babies
How human nature, not technology, causes recurring bubbles
How to protect portfolios from bubbles by focusing on value, not narratives
And so much more!
